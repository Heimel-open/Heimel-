# HEIMEL Open — public/private boundary (decision 2026-10-08)

HEIMEL Open is the **public, implementation-neutral authority-to-consequence contract**: normative requirements, minimal reference semantics, conformance, formal models and public verification evidence.

HEIMEL Open is not the complete VALO platform, nor the private HEIMEL production implementation.

- **Public:** contract, minimal reference adapter, formal models, conformance vectors and release evidence, subject to `PUBLIC_BOUNDARY.json`.
- **Private:** enterprise enforcement implementation, operational integrations, production adapters, internal security tests, proprietary research, and broader VALO product/runtime code.
- **Independent implementations:** may target the published contract under the applicable licenses.
- **Claims:** passing a public conformance suite does not itself establish clinical production safety, external-effect correctness or authorization provenance.

Commercial distinction: HEIMEL Open establishes interoperability and trust; separate private enterprise implementations may offer paid deployment, integration, operations and support. VALO products may use HEIMEL to deliver measurable customer outcomes.

This document does not change the scope of the immutable HEIMEL v0.2.0 release, does not authorize publication of private implementation, and does not supersede `PUBLIC_BOUNDARY.json`.
